About Us

Scam Educator App

SEA (Scam Educator App) was created by Henry Zhang, Tyler Su, Sandra Latt, and Pompie Yannopoulos, students at Manhattan Center for Science and Mathematics in New York City. We built it for the Congressional App Challenge because scams are a growing problem and many people, of all ages, do not know how to spot the warning signs of fraudulent messages, websites, and online interactions. With the rapid advancement of artificial intelligence, deepfakes have made it harder than ever to tell what is real, so we wanted to build a tool that teaches people what to look for and gives them the confidence to stay safe online. SEA walks you through each unit, quizzes you along the way, finishes with a final test, and links to a form where you can report a scam.
